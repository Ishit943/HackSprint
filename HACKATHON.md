## 📌 Problem Statement — PS10

> *"Students struggle to find the right teammates for hackathons and academic projects. There is no dedicated platform for discovering complementary skill sets within a college ecosystem."*

**Monster Match** solves this by providing:
- Smart 100-point compatibility scoring
- Skill-gap–aware complementary teammate discovery
- Privacy-first WhatsApp contact unlock
- Academic email trust verification
- A gamified Halloween monster identity system
