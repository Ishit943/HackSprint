---

## 2. Slide-by-Slide Presentation Structure

### Slide 1: The Hackathon Nightmare (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Problem:**
  - 65% of students attend hackathons solo or drop out because they couldn't find complementary teammates.
  - Discord/WhatsApp group chats are noisy, unorganized, and full of *"who knows React?"* spam.
  - Students end up with 4 frontend devs and zero backend or design coverage.
- **The Solution:** Monster Match — smart, skill-gap-aware teammate matchmaking with zero noise.

### Slide 2: The Monster Match Architecture & Innovation (0:30 - 1:15)
- **100-Point Match Algorithm:** Matches based on skills (70pts), weekly availability (15pts), domain interests (10pts), and proficiency tier bonuses (5pts).
- **Complementary Teammate Discovery:** Identifies team skill gaps (e.g. if you have Backend, it elevates UI/UX and Frontend candidates).
- **Monster Identity:** Fun, deterministic DiceBear Bottts avatars that keep profiles engaging without photo privacy anxiety.
- **Academic Trust Badge:** `.ac.in` and `.edu` verification eliminates spam bots.

### Slide 3: Live Demo Walkthrough (1:15 - 2:15)
- **Step 1:** Log in instantly as Aarav Sharma (Full-Stack Dev) with 1 click.
- **Step 2:** Search on Explore with multi-skill **AND** filter: `Python` + `Machine Learning`.
- **Step 3:** Find Ananya Verma (AI Engineer) with a 94% match score.
- **Step 4:** Send a connection request with a pitch note.
- **Step 5:** Privacy gating in action: Ananya's contact details remain masked until she accepts.
- **Step 6:** Switch to Ananya, click **Accept & Unlock** → WhatsApp direct click-to-chat opens instantly!

### Slide 4: Tech Stack & Engineering Excellence (2:15 - 2:45)
- **Zero Build Complexity:** 100% pure HTML5, CSS custom properties, and native ES6 modules.
- **Dual Pipeline:** Instant client-side `wa.me` deep links + Supabase Edge Functions with Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
- **High Resilience:** 100% offline-capable local mock storage with 6 seeded personas.
- **Security:** PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) + XSS HTML sanitization on all user content.

### Slide 5: Future Roadmap & Vision (2:45 - 3:00)
- AI resume parsing (PDF to skill vectors).
- Devpost / hackathon schedule bots.
- Voice pitches for soft-skill matching.
- **Thank you! Questions?**
